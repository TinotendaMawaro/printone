Executive Profile

Meet Mr. Robin MawaroCEO & Founder of Print One

A results-driven Young Entrepreneur and CEO of Print One, holder of an HND in Management of Information Systems (IMIS). Since establishing Print One in 2021 in Harare, Robin has specialized in printing and branding solutions with a passion for helping businesses stand out through high-quality visual identity materials.

He is highly experienced in delivering creative and professional services such as 3D & 2D Signage, Billboards & Banners, Flyers, T-shirt branding, Packaging, Promotional items, and Corporate branding materials. With a strong eye for detail and commitment to excellence, he combines innovation, quality production, and customer-focused service to meet the branding needs of individuals, startups, and established organizations.

"His goal is to transform ideas into powerful brand expressions that leave lasting impressions and drive business growth. Known for reliability, creativity, and professionalism, he continues to build a reputation as a trusted name in the printing and branding industry."
